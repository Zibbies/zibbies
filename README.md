## 🏢 Account Management & Customer Success

My CX approach extends beyond reactive support into **account health, retention and proactive relationship management**. I look at the customer at the account level:
* Ticket frequency and severity
* Repeat contacts and reopened issues
* SLA performance and product adoption
* Unresolved technical issues
* Customer sentiment and renewal timing
* Commercial importance and growth/expansion opportunity

For high-value and **HNI/customer-sensitive accounts**, I focus on proactive ownership, clear communication, escalation visibility and structured recovery plans.

### Proactive Account Management

Instead of waiting for customers to raise problems, I use customer signals to identify lifecycle stages:

$$\text{Stable} \longrightarrow \text{Watch} \longrightarrow \text{At Risk} \longrightarrow \text{Intervention}$$

This creates a proactive operating rhythm where teams can intervene before recurring issues become a renewal risk, churn, reputation damage, or revenue leakage.

##  The 80/20 Resolution Mindset

I use the **80/20 principle** to identify where customer demand is concentrated and where operational intervention can create the greatest impact. The question is not: *"How do we answer more tickets?"* It is: 

> **"Which small number of recurring problems, accounts, workflows or failure points are creating the largest share of customer effort?"**

This helps prioritize high-volume categories, repeat contacts, engineering escalations, and renewal-risk customers. The result is a shift from **ticket handling → demand reduction → customer retention → scalable CX**.
